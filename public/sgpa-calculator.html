Official SPPU Grading Scale & Conversion Rules

Both the newly revised 2024 Pattern (under NEP-2020) and the 2019 Pattern (CBCS) follow Savitribai Phule Pune University's official 10-point credit scale:

Marks Range Letter Grade Grade Point Performance Level
90 – 100 O 10 Outstanding
80 – 89 A+ 9 Excellent
70 – 79 A 8 Very Good
60 – 69 B+ 7 Good
50 – 59 B 6 Above Average
45 – 49 C 5 Average
40 – 44 P 4 Pass
< 40 F 0 Fail (Backlog / ATKT)
Official SPPU Percentage Conversion Formula: According to SPPU examination circulars, SGPA/CGPA is converted to percentage using the formula:
Percentage (%) = (SGPA − 0.75) × 10
